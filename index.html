Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before you go

Is Bangladesh safe for foreign travelers?+

Yes, Bangladesh is generally very safe for tourists. The locals are incredibly hospitable and friendly. Our guides prioritize your safety at every step, ensuring safe routes, verified accommodations, and constant support throughout your journey.

What is the best time of year to visit?+

The best time to visit is from October to March. During these months, the weather is pleasant with temperatures around 15-25°C, the humidity is lower, and the skies are clear — perfect for beach activities, jungle safaris, and sightseeing.

How does the booking process work?+

Simply fill out the inquiry form below or WhatsApp us. We'll reply within 1-2 hours to confirm availability and answer questions. A small advance payment secures your dates. The remaining balance can be paid in cash (USD/EUR/BDT) upon arrival.

Can dietary restrictions be accommodated?+

Absolutely! Whether you're vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, or have allergies, we can accommodate your dietary needs. Just let us know in the booking form, and our local restaurant partners will prepare meals that are safe and delicious for you.
