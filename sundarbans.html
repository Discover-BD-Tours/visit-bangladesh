About The Sundarbans

The world's largest single block of tidal halophytic mangrove forest

🌴

Mangrove Forests

Spanning 10,000 sq km across Bangladesh and India, the Sundarbans is a vast network of mangrove forests, tidal waterways, and small islands.

🐅

Royal Bengal Tiger

Home to approximately 400 Royal Bengal Tigers, this is one of the few places where tigers live in mangrove ecosystems.

🚣

River Journeys

Explore narrow creeks and wide rivers on traditional boats. The silence of the waterways creates pure magic.

🌳

Wildlife

Beyond tigers, discover spotted deer, crocodiles, wild boars, monkeys, and over 260 bird species in this biodiversity hotspot.

🎣

Fishing Communities

Meet the resilient Munda and Bede communities who've lived in harmony with the forest for generations.

🌿

Ecosystem

A UNESCO World Heritage Site where land meets sea. The Sundarbans protects Bangladesh's coast from cyclones and supports millions of livelihoods.

Wildlife Highlights

Encounter the magnificent creatures of the mangrove forest

Royal Bengal Tiger

The apex predator of the Sundarbans. These majestic cats have adapted to swimming in saline water — a unique evolutionary trait found nowhere else. IUCN Status: Endangered.

Crocodiles

Both saltwater and freshwater crocodiles inhabit these waters. The massive saltwater crocodile can grow up to 6 meters long. Est. population: 200+.

Exotic Birds

Over 260 bird species including kingfishers, herons, egrets, and the majestic white-bellied sea eagle. A paradise for birdwatchers and photographers. Best time: Nov – Feb.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before your safari

Is the Sundarbans safe for tourists?+

Yes, with proper guidance. All our tours include an armed forest guard and an experienced local guide. We stick to approved routes, safe watchtowers, and never venture into core tiger territory without protection. Your safety is our top priority.

Will we see the Royal Bengal Tiger?+

While the Sundarbans hosts around 400 tigers, sightings are rare but possible. Only about 1 in 50 visitors spot one. However, you'll definitely see deer, crocodiles, monkeys, and incredible birdlife. We maximize your chances by visiting known tiger territories at optimal times.

What should I pack for the Sundarbans?+

Pack light, breathable long-sleeved clothing (green/brown colors), sturdy waterproof sandals, wide-brimmed hat, sunscreen (SPF 50+), insect repellent, binoculars, camera with zoom lens, personal medications, and a light rain jacket. We'll send a detailed packing list after booking!

What's the best time to visit?+

November to February is ideal, with pleasant temperatures (15-25°C / 59-77°F), low humidity, and the highest chance of wildlife sightings. The forest is accessible, mosquitoes are fewer, and the weather is perfect for boat safaris and forest walks.
