Ratargul Swamp Forest

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Sylhet

Is Sylhet safe for foreign travelers?+

Absolutely. Sylhet division is one of the safest regions in Bangladesh, with very low crime rates and welcoming local communities. Our guides are experienced in hosting international travelers and ensuring your comfort and safety throughout the journey.

What's the best time to visit the tea gardens?+

October to March is ideal, with pleasant temperatures (18-28°C / 64-82°F) and clear skies perfect for photography and garden walks. The tea gardens remain lush during monsoon (June-September) too, but heavy rains can limit outdoor activities.

What should I pack for Sylhet?+

Pack light, breathable clothing, comfortable walking shoes, rain jacket (rain is common), sunscreen, insect repellent, and a light jacket for cooler mornings. Don't forget your camera, power bank, and binoculars for bird watching!

Can you accommodate dietary preferences?+

Yes! Sylheti cuisine is diverse and we can accommodate vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, and other dietary needs. Just let us know in the booking form. You'll enjoy fresh organic produce, local fish, and unique Sylheti dishes prepared with care.
