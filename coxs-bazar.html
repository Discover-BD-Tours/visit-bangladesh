Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before you go

Is Cox's Bazar safe for foreign travelers?+

Absolutely. Cox's Bazar is one of Bangladesh's most visited tourist destinations with a strong local tourism infrastructure. Our guides are experienced in hosting international travelers, and we prioritize your safety at every step. We avoid crowded areas and stick to safe, verified routes and accommodations.

What is the best time of year to visit?+

The best time to visit Cox's Bazar is from October to March (winter season in Bangladesh). During these months, the weather is pleasant with temperatures around 20-28°C (68-82°F), the humidity is lower, and the sea is calm — perfect for beach activities and sightseeing.

What should I pack for the tour?+

Pack light, breathable clothing, swimwear, sunscreen, a hat, comfortable sandals/slippers, and a light jacket for evenings. Don't forget your camera, power bank, and any personal medications. We'll send you a detailed packing list after booking!

How does the booking process work?+

Simply fill out the inquiry form below. We'll reply within 24 hours via email or WhatsApp to confirm availability and answer any questions. A small advance payment secures your dates. The remaining balance can be paid in cash (USD/EUR/BDT) upon arrival. No credit card required upfront!

Can dietary restrictions be accommodated?+

Yes! Whether you're vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, or have allergies, we can accommodate your dietary needs. Just let us know in the booking form, and our local restaurant partners will prepare meals that are safe and delicious for you.
