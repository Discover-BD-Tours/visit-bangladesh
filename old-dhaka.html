A Journey Through Time

Old Dhaka's story spans centuries of empires and cultures

1565 – 1857

Mughal Era

Old Dhaka was founded by the Mughal Empire under Emperor Akbar. It flourished as a thriving commercial and cultural center, known for its fine muslin, silk, and saltpeter trade. The Mughals built Lalbagh Fort, Ahsan Manzil's precursors, and numerous mosques that still stand today.

1857 – 1947

Colonial Period

Under British rule, Dhaka became a district headquarters of Bengal Presidency. The colonial era brought Victorian architecture, the expansion of educational institutions, and the growth of the jute trade. Railway connections and the development of broader streets changed the city's layout while preserving its historic core.

1947 – Present

Modern Old Dhaka

After Partition and later independence of Bangladesh in 1971, Old Dhaka evolved into a bustling commercial hub while fiercely preserving its heritage. Today, it's a UNESCO World Heritage Site nominee, where ancient traditions coexist with modern Bangladeshi life in a uniquely chaotic, beautiful harmony.

Traditional Food Highlights

Taste the flavors that define Old Dhaka

Kacchi Biryani

The crown jewel of Old Dhaka cuisine. Fragrant rice layered with marinated mutton, potatoes, and aromatic spices, slow-cooked in a sealed pot.

📍 Try at: Haji Biryani, Nanna Biryani

Bakarkhani

A thick, round bread with a crisp exterior and soft interior, sprinkled with sesame seeds. Perfect with tea or paired with kebabs.

📍 Try at: Charmaghaz, Standard Bakery

Street Food

From tangy fuchka (pani puri) to spicy jhalmuri, Old Dhaka's street food scene is vibrant, affordable, and addictive. Every lane has a specialty.

📍 Try at: Chawkbazar Street

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before exploring Old Dhaka

Is Old Dhaka safe for foreign travelers?+

Absolutely. Old Dhaka is a bustling commercial and residential area with plenty of locals around at all times. Our guides are experienced in navigating the area safely and will keep you away from any congested or uncomfortable spots. We stick to well-trodden tourist routes and verified eateries.

What should I wear for the heritage walk?+

We recommend modest, comfortable clothing respecting local culture. Long pants or skirts, and shirts with sleeves are appropriate. Comfortable walking shoes are essential as we'll cover 5-6 km on foot. Bring a scarf if you plan to visit mosques or religious sites.

What's the best time of year to visit Old Dhaka?+

October to March is ideal, with pleasant temperatures (20-28°C / 68-82°F) perfect for walking tours. Summers (April-June) can be very hot and humid, while monsoon (July-September) brings heavy rains that can disrupt the walking tour. Winter mornings are the most comfortable.

Can dietary restrictions be accommodated?+

Yes! Old Dhaka has fantastic vegetarian and vegan options. Just let us know your dietary needs in the booking form, and we'll take you to restaurants that can safely accommodate you. The traditional biryani can be made with vegetables or chicken as alternatives to mutton.
